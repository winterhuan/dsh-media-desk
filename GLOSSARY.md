# Media Desk

English | [中文](GLOSSARY.zh.md)

Media Desk organizes a creator's source material, work, writing preferences and performance review. These terms distinguish the material used to write from the work being produced.

## Language

**Work**: A piece of content the creator develops, edits and schedules, from an initial idea to a finished draft.

_Avoid_: Session, generation request

**Source material**: Text, research, links or media used to develop a work. Source material is not automatically the creator's own writing or an adopted factual claim.

_Avoid_: Personal style, finished draft

**Production**: The script, title, cover, illustrations and storyboard prepared for a work. A production does not imply that a video has been edited or a post published.

_Avoid_: Published post, finished video

**Style card**: An editable description of a creator's writing characteristics, derived from selected samples or supplied by the user.

_Avoid_: Knowledge summary, source material

**Writing rule**: A preference used when writing, such as tone or length. It is distinct from a DSH skill's instructions and tools.

_Avoid_: Skill bundle, knowledge source

**Performance review**: Analysis of recorded publication and interaction data, together with the creator's draft edits. It does not establish that a writing choice caused a platform result.

_Avoid_: Platform crawler, model training
